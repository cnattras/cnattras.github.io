We used Marco's parameterization for v2 for the v2 for AuAu200.  Details of the v2 used for all data sets are discussed in Section E of the paper and in greater detail in Chapter 2 Section 2.2.2 of the analysis note.  We used fairly large systematic errors and we think that this approach is pretty conservative - that is, we think we have included physically reasonable values for v2.  The details vary by data set, but all systems include a method like v2{FTPC} which may still have significant non-flow effects and a method like v2{4} which may underestimate v2.

If anything ZYAM will underestimates the ridge yield.  ZYAM is applied consistently for all data sets and the goal is to compare different collision systems and energies.  Systematic errors should be somewhat correlated between different systems so the physics that we are trying to get out of this analysis - the trend for the ridge at different collision energies and systems - is fairly robust to different v2's and to ZYAM.

We have tried to acknowledge the issues with ZYAM and the two-component model in the text, but we don't think that ZYAM's limitations prevent us from drawing some conclusions from the data.  We welcome suggestions for ways to improve the text.
