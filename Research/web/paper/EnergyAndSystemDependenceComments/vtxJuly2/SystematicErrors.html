Discussion

Clearly the statisical errors on the Au+Au amd Cu+Cu  62 are much greater than any effect.

In the Au+Au200 there is a clear centrality dependence.  In all centralities other than 0-12% the difference between the yield for lowest z and highest z is never more than twice the statistical error and the difference in the widths is never more than the statistical error.  The difference in the yield and widths also do not consistently have the same sign.  The trend with vtx z is also not clear for any other centrality; unlike the central data, the order of the heights of the tails do not have a consistent trend with vtx z.

In Cu+Cu 200 the width also shows no significant effect.  The yield may or may not, but like the more peripheral Au+Au data there is no clear trend with vtx z.

The d+Au was not investigated because it would take more changes in the code used to do these checks.

Remember that the histograms are fixed at the peak.  There is a trend for a higher yield at low |vtx z|, however, this can be explained by the higher efficiency for lower |vtx z|.  Given this, there is no evidence for this effect in data sets other than the most central Au+Au 200.
