Minutes of the 1st GPC meeting for Energy/system size dependence of jet-like correlations and the ridge
November 10, 2010
Participants: Lijuan, Fuqiang, Paul, Hua, Christine, Jana


Christine gave a presentation summarizing the main physics results of the paper for jet-like correlations and the ridge, v2 parametrization used in the paper to subtract the elliptic flow background, the limitations of the ZYAM method, outcome of 2D fits requested by Lanny during the jetcorr PWG paper draft review, model discussion and comparison to ISR 62 GeV p+p data and new p+p CMS results at LHC. The main comments were:

1. v2 parametrization:

   a) for Au+Au 200 GeV data the paper uses Marco's v2 parametrization
(http://www.star.bnl.gov/protected/jetcorr/mvl/v2_compare/auau_compare.html)

  and this parametrization has also been used  in other recent correlation papers. Paul asked on which v2 determination methods the parametrization is based on, we discussed differences to Fuqiang's older v2 parametrization.

   b) for Cu+Cu 200 GeV data the paper  uses v2 parametrization from Shusu/Na Li and its syst. errors are significantly smaller in comparison to those for Au+Au 200 GeV data.

    Paul brought up that similar methods should be used in both v2 parametrizations due to different sensitivity of various methods to non-flow effects and that this is crucial in order to have v2 and its syst. errors determined in a consistent way accross collision systems and energies. PA's will contact Shusu and Na Li to provide more details on their parametrization and systematic error determination.

2. ZYAM method: we discussed whether or not to expand the section on limitations of ZYAM. We will concentrate on this topic during the next GPC meeting.

3. 2D fit a la estruct group in central Au+Au 200 GeV data in 0-12% centrality bin gives v22 < 0 which is unphysical. Paul suggested to add a v3 component to the fit which would then pull the v2 term to positive values. PA's will calculate the first three Fourier components of the 2D fit function in the most central bin and extract v3/v2 ratio. This will also serve as a comparison to Roland/Alver paper on triangular flow in heavy-ion collisions. We also discussed the physics of the cos(delta(phi)) term.

4. For 62 GeV Au+Au data, the two most peripheral data points on the ridge yield are negative. This is probably due to the too large v2 values used in elliptic flow subtraction. It is not clear whether we should remove these data points from Fig. 13.

5. In Table II, Nbin values for 0-10, 10-40 and 40-80% centrality bins in Au+Au 62 GeV are missing. We discussed, that unfortunately the published papers, where Nbin is quoted, used different centrality binning. PA's will contact Jamie etc. and ask for help (evt. run the Glauber calculation).

6. Paul asked whether the raw correlation functions will be put on the STAR webpage once the paper gets released to public. The answer was yes. Paul also suggested to add a table on the web with the number of trigger particles for each data point in the paper. PA's will prepare this table.

7. Lijuan asked Hua about the QA of the analysis code. Hua will look into this next week. PA's will provide him with more details if necessary and also will point Hua to the root trees location at RCF, so the code could be tested on real physics data.
