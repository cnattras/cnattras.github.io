GPC minutes 2 Mar. 2011

Christine looked at the v2, v3, and v4 for the acceptance correction to see what possible systematic errors due to a poor acceptance correction would be.  This takes the extreme case that there would be no acceptance correction applied.  The results of this exercise are:
v2 -0.0244727% v3 -0.0188528% v4 0.00769827%
This is well below the statistical uncertainty on the vn from the fits (two orders of magnitude) and is therefore negligible

Fuqiang does not approve of comparisons of (v3/v2)^2 to theory calculations.  Everyone else does.

Consensus that we will move forward with a note in the report from the GPC that Fuqiang disagrees with the model comparison.

Three specific things should be  addressed in the text
  • Lijuan requests that we add a sentence stating that at some point in the future both theory and experiment should attempt to address systematic uncertainties due to correlations from other sources.
  • Lijuan requests that when discussing the models we state that the three models we compare to also need to be compared to spectra and v2 measurements too to make sure they are consistent with other data
  • Fuqiang requests that we word the statements about how (tilde-v3/tilde-v2)^2 would be larger in a way such that it makes it clear that this is based on the assumption that any non-flow contributions contribute the same way as d+Au
