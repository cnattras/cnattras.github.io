Report of the Referee -- CK10308/Agakishiev
----------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper presents interesting new data on the systematic in the
properties of jet-like yield and the near-side ridge component in
heavy- ion collisions as a function of beam energy and system size. As
noted, some of the results are somewhat unexpected, e.g. the fact that
PYTHIA reproduced so well the Cu+Cu and Au+Au jet like data and the
observation that there is no significant difference between the
jet-like yield data for d+Au, Cu+Cu and Au+Au collisions for a given
number of participants. I believe these data will have an impact and
be used by theorists with interests in high-energy heavy-ion
reactions. In particular, and as well explained in the discussion such
data could be useful to evaluate the various models for the production
of the ridge that is largely not understood. The paper is well written
with a good discussion of the methodology used in data analysis and
the corrections for the possible source of bias in this complex
analysis. I thus recommend that the paper be published as is. I found
only one minor mistake on line 13 of page 8. It is written ".. by a
factor of 1.6 in sqrt(SNN) =200 GeV relative to 62 GeV.". I believe it
should read ".. by a factor of 1.6 at sqrt(SNN) =200 GeV relative to
62 GeV." or ".. by a factor of 1.6 in sqrt(SNN) =200 GeV collisions
relative to 62 GeV.".

We have changed this wording to
"..a factor of 1.6 in collisions at sqrt(SNN) = 200 relative to collisions at sqrt(SNN) = 62."
