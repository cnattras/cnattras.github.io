Systematic errors:

There is no systematic error on (v3/v2)^2 due to the efficiency because the any uncertainty in the efficiency would change the magnitude of the modulations.

To determine the effect of the acceptance correction on v3 and v2, we calculated the v2 and v3 of the acceptance correction.  This is essentially assuming a 100% error.  The v2 of the acceptance correction is -0.049% and the v3 of the acceptance correction is -0.037%.

In order to determine the systematic error due to the fit method, we varied the yield and the width within the limit given in the systematic errors for those quantities, 9% and 6%, respectively.  As with the other systematic errors, we use the Au+Au 200 as the worst case

The largest difference is a diffference of 0.02, or a difference of 3.5%.  To be conservative, we round to 4% and assume that the systematic error is symmetric.
