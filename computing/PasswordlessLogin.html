setting up password-less login to the nodes

1.  Log in to rhig
2.  Type "ssh-keygen -t rsa".  Accept all defaults and do not enter a password.
3.  Type "cat ~/.ssh/id_rsa.pub >> ~/.ssh/authorized_keys"
4.  Log in and out of every node (irhig02,irhig03,irhig04,irhig05,irhig06,irhig07)

Now you should be able to log in to every node without a password.
