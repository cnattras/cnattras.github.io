How to print on Yale letterhead

Leave two blank lines on the top of the document.

Place the paper one sheet at a time face up with "Yale University" close to you and the bottom of the paper facing the printer in tray one (the tray that pulls out.)  If you place multiple sheets of letterhead in the tray, it may pick up more than one sheet. 

If you have a multipage document, once paper runs out, the printer will draw all following pages from the second tray.  You can put fancy paper for later pages in tray 2.  Sheets placed in tray 2 should have "Yale University" on the side close to the printer and should be placed face down in tray 2.
