Aunt Marlene's Whole Wheat Bread

1 cake fresh yeast or 1 pkg. granular yeast
2 cups lukewarm water
2 Tablespoons sugar
2 teaspoons salt
3 cups white flour
1/2 cup hot water
1/2 cup brown sugar
3 Tablspoons shortening
3 cups Whole Wheat flour

Soften yeast in lukewarm water.  Add sugar, salt and white flour.  Beat smooth.  Set in warm place until light and bubbly.  Combine hot water with brown sugar and shortening.  Cool to luke warm.  Add to sponge.  Add whole wheat flour.  Mix smooth, knead and let rise.  Place in loaf pan.  Let rise until doubled.  Bake in moderate over (375) for 50 minutes.  Makes two 1 pound loaves.

Honey Corn Bread

1 cup flour
1/4 cup sugar
1/2 teaspoon salt
1 cup whipping cream
1/4 cup honey
1 cup cornmeal
1 Tablespoon baking powder
2 eggs
1/4 cup vegetable oil

Combine dry ingredients.  Beat eggs, and then add cream and honey.  Beat mixture well.  Stir in dry ingredients.  Pour into a 9 inch square pan.  Bake at 400 degrees for 25 minutes.

Grandma Doll's dinner rolls

 Dissolve 2 Tablespoons of yeat (or 2 envelopes) into 1/2 cup of warm water.  Heat 2 cups of milk.  Add 1 and 1/2 sticks of butter and melt.   Add 2 teaspoons salt and 1/2 cup sugar to the milk/butter mix.  Add 3 eggs and yeast/water mix.  Finally, add enough flour to make soft dough.   Let rise once.  Then shape and let rise again.  Bake at 350 degrees for 15-20 minutes.

Ginger snaps

3/4 cup shortening
1/4 cup molasses
1 cup sugar
1 egg
2 teaspoons soda
2 cups flour
1/2 teaspoon cloves
1/2 teaspoon ginger
1 teaspoon cinnamon
1/2 teaspoon salt.  

Melt shortening, remove from heat and let cool.  Add sugar, molasses and egg.  Beat well. Sift flour, soda, cloves, ginger, cinnamon, and salt.  Add to first mixture and mix well.  Chill.  Form into balls, roll in surgar, place on greased cookie sheet.  Bake 375 degrees for 8-10 minutes.  Best taste comes from making these with bacon grease for the shortening but, of course, they're not as healthy that way!

Scottish shortbread

1 cup sugar
1 pound butter
1 teaspoon water
4 cups flour
2 teaspoons almond extract

Add water to sugar.  Add butter and mix well with your hands.  Add flour and mix well with your hands.  Add almond extract.  Pat evenly into large cookie sheet with sides.  Lightly prick all over with fork.  Bake at 360 to 380 degrees for about 20 minutes until it begins to turn golden.  Let cool a few minutes, and cut into small squares.  Cool thoroughly before removing from the pan

German potato soup

7 slices lean bacon
3 large onions, finely chopped
4 1/2 Tablespoons flour
9 cups beef broth
6 large potatoes, peeled and sliced thinly
3 egg yolks
1 1/2 cups sour cream

Dice the bacon and saute it in a deep kettle for 5 minutes.  Stir in chopped onions and saute until soft (approx. five minutes).  Blend in flour and gradually add broth.  Add sliced potatoes to simmering broth and cook for 1 hour.  Beat the egg yolks with the sour cream.  Stir 1/2 cup of hot soup into the egg yolk mixture and add this to the soup.  Simmer ten minutes more, stirring constantly.  Serve immediately.


Cowboy Beef Stew

2 pounds beef stew meat, cut into 1 inch cubes
2 Tablespoons butter
1 onion, finely chopped
flour
1 cup broth
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/4 teaspoon pepper
1/2 teaspoon savory
Pinch of oregano
1 teaspoon worcestershire sauce
1 teaspoon prepared mustard
Juice of 1/2 lemon
1/2 teaspoon parsley

Saute onion in butter.  Dust beef pieces with flour, add to onion and brown thoroughly,  Stir in the broth.  Season with salt, pepper, savory and oregano.  Simmer for one hour until meat is tender.  Add worcestershire, mustard, and lemon juice and cook 10 minutes more.  Add parsley and serve.


Corned Beef Hash

2 pounds cooked corn beef, chopped
4 boiled potatoes, chopped
1 small onion, minced
salt
1/4 teaspoon pepper
2 Tablespoons butter
1/4 cup cream

Mix corned beef and freshly cooked potatoes.  There should be twice as much corned beef as potatoes.  Add salt and pepper and onion.  Fry in butter in a very hot, heavy skillet.  Reduce heat and cook slowly, adding creanm now and then to keep moist.  Brown the top under a broiler or turn bottom-side-up on a platter.


Buttermilk pancakes

1 egg
1 cup buttermilk
2 Tablespoons melted butter
1 cup flour
1 Tablespoon sugar
1 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon baking soda

Beat egg.  Add remaining ingredients in the order listed, and beat gently just until flour is moist.  Pour batter onto hot griddle.  Turn pancakes once they're full of bubbles but before bubbles break.  Bake 2nd side until golden brown.

To make buckwheat pancakes, substitute a flour mixture that is half white and half buckwheat.


Scalloped potatoes and ham

6 medium potatoes, peeled and sliced thinly
3 Tablespoons butter
3 Tablespoons flour
salt and pepper
1 1/2 cups cubed ham

2 1/2 cups milk
1/4 cup finely chopped onion
1 Tablespoon butter

Melt butter in saucepan over low heat.  Blend in flour and seasonings.  Stir in milk.  Heat to boiling, stirring constantly.  Boil and stir for 1 minute.  In greased 2 quart casserole, arrange potatoes in two layers, topping each with half the onion and 1/3  of the white sauce.  Top with remaining potatoes and sauce.  Dot with 1 Tablespoon butter.  Cover and bake for 30 minutes in 350 degree oven.  Uncover and bake 60 minutes longer.  Let stand 5-10 minutes before serving.
