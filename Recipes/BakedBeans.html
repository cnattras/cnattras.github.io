Christine's Baked beans 7/10

1/2 pound navy beans, soaked overnight.  Boil 10 minutes.  Drain.
Mix with:
1 small chopped onion
2 cloves garlic
1/4 c brown sugar
1/4 c molasses
1 tsp salt
1c chicken broth

cook in crock pot until soft (4-6 hours on high)

Source: me.

Notes:  Be sure to precook the beans enough because the sugar will prevent them from getting really soft.
