Tiramisu 6/10

10 eggs
about 1 kilogram of biscuits (the Italian hard biscuits like biscotti)
1/2 kilo sugar
espresso (to dunk biscuits in)
mascarpone (Italian cream cheese) - 1 kg

take the egg whites and whip until they form peaks that fall.
Add the egg yolks and sugar
mix
add mascarpone

then layer in this order:  Cream, biscuits, cream, biscuits...

refridgerate 1-2 hours
can sprinkle cocoa on top

Source:  Italian physicists I worked with at CERN

Notes:  This is not the best tiramisu I've ever had but it is good.  I marked it down largely because my
microbiological training makes me try to avoid eating raw eggs.
