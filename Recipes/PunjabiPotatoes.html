Mild Punjabi potatoes

6 russet potatoes, peeled

1 29 oz can diced tomatoes

1/2 bulb garlic

1 tbsp tumeric

1 tbsp papricka

2 tbsp cumin

1 tsp salt

1/2 serrano

1 onion

puree all but potatoes

handful of methi leaves

3 tbsp butter

Mix in crock pot and cook 12 hours on low

Note this cuts the ginger entirely.

This is roughly 1/2 potatoes and half veggies.

Adapted from Crockpot Indian Cooking both to make it milder (it always came out waaaaaay too hot even for me) and to use canned tomatoes.
