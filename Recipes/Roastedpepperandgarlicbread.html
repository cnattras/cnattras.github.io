Roasted pepper and garlic bread

2 cups sour dough starter
6 cups of white unbleached flour
1/2 c olive oil
2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
1 tbsp salt
2 tbsp butter, cut into smaller pieces

water to make right consistency - about 3/4 c.  Mix together - dough should be just shy of sticky.  Let rise for two hours.  While rising:

Roast two red bell peppers.  Turn the stove burner on high.  Slice the bell peppers into the largest possible flat-ish pieces.  Place 3-4 peppers at a time on the burner and roast for about five minutes, turning frequently with tongs.  Black spots should form on the peppers but they should not be completely blackened.  As the pepper pieces are roasted, put them on a small plate.  Once all of the pepper pieces are roasted, cover the plate with foil and let sit at least 20 minutes.  (This steams the peppers so they actually get soft.)  Before the bread is done rising, cut the peppers into pieces about 1/4" by 1".  (See http://www.ehow.com/how_13875_roast-peppers.html)

Roast one bulb of garlic.  Turn the oven on to 350F.  Take the outer layer of husks off the garlic bulb - but don't take all of it off, leave the bulb intact.  Cut off the tips of the garlic cloves, leaving the garlic exposed.  Cover the exposed part in olive oil.  Roast for 30 minutes in the oven.  Let cool.  (See http://elise.com/recipes/archives/001712roasted_garlic.php)  Once the garlic has cooled, take the remaining husk off.  Slice each clove.  Mix with the olive oil it was roasted in.

Toast sesame seeds.  Take about 1/2 c of sesame seeds and put them in a frying pan with no oil on medium head.  Roast for about 4-5 minutes, until sesame seeds are lightly browned.  Let cool.

Once dough has risen, knead for about five minutes.  Then add about 2/3 of the sesame seeds, garlic, and peppers.  Mix in thoroughly.  Divide the dough into two pieces and shape into an oblong shape.  Cover a baking sheet completely with a layer of flour.  Take each loaf, rough thoroughly in flour, and place on the baking sheet.  Cover in sesame seeds.  Let rise about 40 minutes.  Then bake about 30 minutes on 425F.
