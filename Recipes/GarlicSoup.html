Garlic Soup
about 1 L water from boiled potatoes (this is a great thing to do with water from boiled potatoes)
2-3 cloves crushed garlic
1 tsp dill seed, crushed
1/2 tsp salt
1 tsp marjoram
1 tsp vegetable boullion

Boil about 5 minutes

Source: Rada Chvala

Notes:  This sounds a little weird at first but it is good, and it's a great thing to do with any water leftover from boiling potatoes.
