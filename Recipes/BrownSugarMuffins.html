Brown Sugar Muffins 9/10

1 1/2 c flour
1/4 c granulated sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1/2 tsp cinnamon
3 1/2 tsp baking powder
1/4 c light brown sugar
1 egg, lighly beaten
1/2 c oil
1/2 c milk
preheat oven to 400F
sift together flour, granulated sugar, salt, and baking powder.
combine the egg, oil, and milk and stir into dry ingredients just enough to moisten.
Then add the brown sugar and cinnamon.  Don't stir too much -- there should still be
visible chunks of sugar separated from the rest of the batter.
fill greased muffin tins 2/3 full and bake 20-25 min or until done
makes about 18 muffins

Notes:  These are one of my favorites.  They might be a little sweet for some people's tastes.
