Blender drinks

mocha
3 tbsp toddy (take coarsely ground coffee, cover in 3x the ground coffee's volume water, let soak 8 hours, and strain the grounds away)
7 ice cubes
2 tbsp cocoa
3 tbsp sugar
milk to cover ice

peaches and cream
1 peach, pitted and chopped coarsely
3 tbsp cream
2 tbsp sugar
6 pitted cherries
5 ice cubes

cherry lemonade
1/4 c lemon juice (from 3/4 lemon)
1/4 c sugar
11 cherries
7 ice cubes
1 c water
