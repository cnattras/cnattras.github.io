Nakladany Hermelin - Marinated Hermelin - an adaption
1 lb of camembert
3 serrano chiles
3 Fresno chiles
1 red bell pepper
2 onions
1 bulb garlic
lots of olive oil (about 0.75 L)

Stick the camembert in the freezer for about 20 minutes.  This will get it to firm up a bit.  Heat a knife under hot water.  Slice the camembert in 1/2 and cut the camembert into chunks about 1-1.5cm^3.  Dice the chiles finely.  Peel and finely chop the bulb of garlic.  Wash the bell pepper and cut into pieces about 1 cm^2.  Peel and slice the onion finely.  Mix everything together and cover in olive oil inside a vacuum sealable container.  Make a vacuum seal as tight as possible.  Let sit for a few weeks.
