Grandma Watson's Sugar Cookies
1 cup sugar
1 cup powdered sugar
1 cup butter
1 cup salad oil
 2 eggs
4 cups and 4 teaspoons flour
1 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon vanilla
1 teaspoon soda
1/4 teaspoon almond extract
1 teaspoon cream of tartar.

Beat two sugars, butter, and oil.  Add eggs.  Then combine dry ingredients and add.  Roll in balls, roll balls in sugar, and arrange on cookie sheet.  Flatten gently with a fork or glass.   Bake at 375 degrees until lightly brown.
