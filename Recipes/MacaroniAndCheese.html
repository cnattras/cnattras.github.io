Macaroni and cheese

16 oz package of macaroni

10 oz package of sharp cheddar cheese, shredded

1 c half and half

1/2 bulb garlic - crushed

2.5 tbsp butter

1.5 tsp salt

2 tsp paprika

1 tsp cayenne pepper

1.5 tsp black pepper


Cook macaroni.  Set aside.  Brown garlic in butter.  Add spices while browning butter.  Add half and half and heat until warm.  Add cheese.  Cook, stirring continuously, until smooth.  Add macaroni.
