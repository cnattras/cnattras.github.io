Gingerbread house

We used this recipe.  Note it made about 3x the amount of gingerbread we needed for one house.  I didn't have ginger (!) so we just didn't use ginger.  We also substituted sorghum for the molasses.  It turned out well.

We did not make royal icing the normal way.  I searched online and found that gelatin would substitute for raw egg white - this avoids issues with salmonella.  1 packet of unflavored gelatin + 1/4 cup boiling water, let cool to room temperature = substitute for one egg.  It worked perfectly.
