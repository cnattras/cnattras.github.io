Steamed taters 5/10
4 small potatoes, chopped into roughly 2" cubes
3 cloves garlic
1/4 sauteed onion
1 tsp salt
1 tsp pepper
1 tbsp parsley
1 tbsp cilantro

Combine everything and place in rice steamer, about 1/2 covered in water.  Steam until soft.
