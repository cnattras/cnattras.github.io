Grandma Doll Rolls
heat 2 c milk
add 1 1/2 sticks margarine and melt
dissolve 2 tbsp of yeast in 1/2 c warm water
add 2 tsp salt and a little more than 1/2 c sugar to milk
  - butter.  add enough flour to make batter then add yeast
add 3 eggs when making batter
let rise once, then shape and let rise again
bake at 350F for 15-20 minutes

This is from my great Aunt Babe Doll.
