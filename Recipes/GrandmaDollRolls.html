Grandma Doll Rolls 7/10
heat 2 c milk
add 1 1/2 sticks margarine and melt
dissolve 2 tbsp of yeast in 1/2 c warm water
add 2 tsp salt and a little more than 1/2 c sugar to milk
  - butter.  add enough flour to make batter then add yeast
add 3 eggs when making batter
let rise once, then shape and let rise again
bake at 350F for 15-20 minutes

Source:  This is from my great Aunt Babe Doll.  She came up with them on her own.

Notes:  The reason I marked these down is that they are a little too fluffy for my tastes.  All my time in Europe gave me a taste for
heavier breads, the kind you could punt across a football field.  The flavor is wonderful, however.
