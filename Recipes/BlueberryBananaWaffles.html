Blueberry banana waffles

1 c flour

1 tsp baking powder

1 tsp salt

2 ripe bananas

2 eggs

about 1 c buttermilk

1-1.5 c blueberries


Mix dry ingredients well.  Squish bananas and crack eggs.  Be sure to break the yolks.  Add buttermilk until consistency is right and mix well.  Add blueberries and mix well.


Heat waffle iron up thoroughly (about 3 minutes).  Cook for 3-4 minutes until cooked through.
