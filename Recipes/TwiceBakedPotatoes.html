Twice baked potatoes 10/10

potatoes
cheese (cheddar, Mexican quesa fresca, or provalone all work well)
salt
pepper
sour cream
whipping cream
chives

Bake as many potatoes as you want to make into twice baked potatoes.
Cut in 1/2 and scoop out the insides. 
For every 2 pounds of potatoes:
    add about 1/2 c whipping cream
    about 1/2 c sour cream
    about 1 tsp salt
    about 1 tsp pepper
mash the potatoes until they are smooth
add
    about 1/2 pound shredded cheese
    about 1/4 c chives

taste while you're adding ingredients
