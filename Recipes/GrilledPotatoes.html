Grilled potatoes

5 pounds red potatoes, chopped into chunks no more than 1" in any dimension
about 1/3 c olive oil
1 1/2 tsp marjoram
1 1/2 tsp thyme
1 1/2 tsp rosemary
2 tsp salt
mix.  Bake at 375F for 1 hr on a baking sheet, turning every 10 minutes.
