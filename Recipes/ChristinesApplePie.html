Christine's Apple pie

melt butter and cook w/ flour
    1/3 c butter cut into little chunks
    2 tbsp flour
   

Add
    6 apples skin on, chopped
    3/4 c sugar
    1 tsp almond extract
    1 tsp cinnamon

Put in
    pie crust
and add top if you will put a top on

sprinkle on top
    1/4 c b sugar

Cook for 30-45 min at 350.
