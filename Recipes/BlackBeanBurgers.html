Black bean burgers
 
One 12 oz bag of dry black beans
Two bulbs of garlic
Two yellow onions
One red onion
4 red bell peppers
Salt
3 cups bread crumbs
Three eggs
 
Roast the red peppers on a grill until the outside is blackened and blistered. Let cool. Scrape off the outer skin and take the seeds out of the middle. Dice into quarter inch square or smaller pieces.
 
Soak the black beans overnight. Strain (discarding the water*), rinse and put in an instant pot. Peel the two yellow onions and one of the bulbs of garlic and put them in the instant pot. They do not need to be crushed or otherwise prepared. In fact it is easier if you do not because you're going to fish them out later.  Add water to the highest level. Cook the beans in instant pot for 5 minutes and then allow the pressure to naturally release. Let the beans cool until they're cool enough they will not cook the eggs.
 

Peel and dice or crush the remaining bulb of garlic. Dice The red onion into small pieces. Mix the beans, the eggs, and the bread crumbs until you get a paste about the consistency of ground hamburger. Mix in the diced garlic, the diced onion, and the roasted red peppers. Shape into hamburger patties. Should make about 16.

Fry in olive oil for about 6 minutes on each side on medium high.

 

*The reason you discard the water is because it gets rid of more of the stuff that makes you fart.
